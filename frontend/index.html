Trust and safety

A real trust layer — not a decorative badge

Buyer protection starts with transparency. Here is exactly what the platform does, and what it will never claim to do for you.

Every listing reviewed first

New adverts enter a moderation queue and only go public after a human review. Fake and duplicate adverts are rejected.

Verification is separate from payment

A provider registration fee buys directory eligibility — it never buys a verified badge. Verification is granted only after review.

Ratings and reviews that persist

Reputation is built from real interactions and stays on the account, so a history you can check beats a promise you cannot.

Report and block in one tap

Suspicious listings, users and messages can be reported into a moderation queue, and any account can be blocked instantly.

Safety guidance on every listing

Meet in busy public places, inspect before you pay, and never send money or share bank codes with someone you have not met.

Personal data stays private

Phone numbers are released only inside the app and only to signed-in users. Your email is never shown on a public listing.

No platform can guarantee a transaction. Campus Marketplace gives you verified process, visible history and clear safety guidance — the judgement is always yours.

Good to know

Questions people ask before joining

Is Campus Marketplace free to use?

Yes. Creating an account and posting listings is free, and there is no commission on any sale. Optional paid promotion exists purely to give a listing more visibility for a fixed period — it never affects whether a listing is allowed on the platform.

How do I know a listing is genuine?

Every listing is reviewed by a moderator before it appears publicly, and accounts can be verified. Even so, always inspect an item in person and never send money before you have seen what you are buying.

Do I have to be a student to use it?

No. Campus Marketplace is open to everyone in Lafia — students, residents, traders, landlords, agents, organisers and service providers. Students are simply a large part of the community.

How do service providers get listed?

Register as a provider, submit your services and pay the one-time registration fee, then a moderator reviews and approves your profile. Approval and verification are separate steps — paying the fee does not verify you.

What should I do if something looks suspicious?

Report the listing or the account, then block them if you want no further contact. Meet in busy, public places, take a friend if you can, and never share sensitive details like bank codes or PINs.
