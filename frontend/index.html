Good to know

Questions people ask before joining

Is Lafia Marketplace free to use?

Yes. Creating an account and posting listings is free. There is no commission on any sale — buyers and sellers deal with each other directly.

How do I know a listing is genuine?

Every listing is reviewed by a moderator before it appears publicly, and accounts can be verified. Even so, always inspect an item in person and never send money before you have seen what you are buying.

Do I have to be a FULAFIA student?

No. Lafia Marketplace is open to everyone in Lafia — students, residents, traders, landlords, agents and service providers. Students simply make up a large part of the community.

How do I advertise a room or a service?

Choose Hostel / Lodge or Service when you post, add a photo, set your price and describe what you are offering. Your advert goes live once a moderator has reviewed it.

What should I do if something looks suspicious?

Report the listing and contact our moderators. Meet in busy, public places, take a friend if you can, and never share sensitive details like bank codes or PINs.
